OrderGuard · System One · source of truth

System One architecture map

System One answers one question for every thing an AI trading agent does: would this owner endorse it, given what the agent legitimately knew at the time? It answers with a calibrated probability, a named cause that points at evidence, and allow, hold or ask under a stated error bound. It can only make things stricter. Code is the only thing that says yes.

It is not one model. It is a chain of exact code, a recorded reasoning graph, one self-hosted reader, classical statistics and a rare tighten-only judge. Each part below says what it is, why it exists, what we chose, what we rejected, what it learns from and what evidence we have.

The decision

What System One is, decided

  1. Shape: a hybrid cascade. Exact code and the why-graph engine decide every formal truth. One self-hosted decision model reads evidence. A monotone combiner and conformal risk control turn signals into allow, hold or ask. A larger model judges the rare unclear case and can only tighten. (D11, supersedes D10)
  2. The agent's own words are hypotheses, never evidence. Every factual sentence in the agent's reasoning becomes a claim node. With no anchor in a document it is unproven, and an agent cannot cite its own earlier note to make it proven. (D14)
  3. The why graph carries two channels: trust (who it came from) and fidelity (whether the meaning survived). Every claim gets a proof status. The claim an action leans on most must be proven or supported, for every action type, not only buys. (D15)
  4. Hot-path reader: Clef-flash 9B, frozen, calibrated, then head-trained. It is within 0.02 AUROC of the hosted reader on every question in G1. The larger sibling that wins G1 becomes the escalation judge and the teacher. (D12)
  5. Combiner: monotone gradient-boosted trees with TreeSHAP. Calibration: temperature per question, then conformal risk control per action class at α = 0.05. (D13)
  6. Readers see evidence cards, not prose. Agent and third-party text reaches a model only as quoted, typed data. No generative model sits in the decision path except the parsed, tighten-only judge. (D16, D17)
  7. Training order: calibration, then head training with contrastive minimal pairs, then LoRA only if it adds 0.03 or more. No RL on the verdict, no full fine-tuning, nothing from scratch. (D19)

02 · The why graph

How claims propagate, and where they go wrong

The harness records, at the moment of action, every document, note, claim, thesis and action, and which cites which. System One reads only the causal view model_view_at(episode, i). The engine walks this graph before any model reads a word.

Worked example · one action, four claims, one hold

Trust badges are the 0–4 provenance scale used by the engine (4 is a valid owner signature). Under today's T14 rule an agent note starts at 2. Under D14 an agent note or claim with no document anchor drops to 1, so C3 is unproven instead of quietly trusted.

R1
Trust only falls along a chain

Each node's trust is the minimum of its own provenance and everything it cites, directly or through a chain. Least fixed point, so cycles are safe. Built (T14).

R2
Meaning is a second channel

Each claim-to-source edge carries a fidelity score: code for numbers, units, periods and entities; F4 for meaning. A claim's fidelity is the minimum along its chain.

R3
The agent's words are hypotheses

The recorder turns every factual sentence in the agent's rationale into a claim node. With no document anchor it starts at trust 1. An agent note cannot raise the trust of a claim that cites it.

R4
Repetition adds no support

Support and exposure are grouped by root source. One feed behind several "independent" notes or agents is one source, capped in code (single-root cap, T14).

R5
Authority comes only from a signature

Permission exists only as a valid owner signature, checked by code. Text asserting permission is a signal, never a permission. The check covers buys, sells, rebalances, thesis changes and owner requests (T14 checked only buys).

R6
The decisive-claim gate

The claim with the largest share of the action's support must be proven or supported. Otherwise hold above 0.5% of book, or ask the owner where it is a preference call. Independent of any model score.

R7
Nothing from the future

Only documents published and notes written before the action exist. A regression test enforces it (T01).

R8
Case memory

Once a node is found poisoned, stale or reinterpreted, every later action that leans on it inherits the finding. This is code, not model memory, so long error chains are caught at the root.

07 · Decision register

The decisions this map establishes

Recorded in DECISIONS.md. D1–D9 stand (endorsement as target, tighten-only, provenance at decision time, judge theses and check orders in code, two calibration targets). The founder can overrule any entry.

D11
Hybrid cascade

Code and the why-graph engine decide formal truths; one self-hosted decision model reads evidence; monotone combiner; conformal risk control; a tighten-only judge for ≤ 15% of actions; per-owner adjustment. Supersedes the provisional D10.

No learned reader beats code-only by ≥ 0.03 coverage on CS-1000 evidential cells (ship code + calibration). Or the judge adds < 0.10 recall on novel families (drop the judge).

D12
Clef-flash 9B is the hot-path reader

Self-hosted, frozen, calibrated, then head-trained. The hosted reader is kept only as a shadow comparator. The larger G1 winner is the judge and the teacher.

A larger sibling beats it by > 0.02 after calibration on G2 or CS-1000 evidential cells (quality first: move the big model to the hot path). Or binding errors stay < 0.80 after head training (build the X6 verifier).

D13
Monotone boosted trees + TreeSHAP combine the signals

Temperature per reader question, Mondrian conformal per action class, α = 0.05. EBM is kept as an audit comparator.

The EBM beats the trees by > 0.02 AUROC or 0.03 ECE on the unseen worlds (X1).

D14
Agent words are hypotheses

Every factual sentence in the agent's reasoning becomes a claim node. Unanchored agent notes and claims start at trust 1. Self-citation cannot raise trust.

The rule holds more than 1 in 100 clean actions in CS-1000 and the unseen worlds, and anchoring at the harness cannot fix it.

D15
Two-channel why graph, proof status, decisive-claim gate

Trust and fidelity both propagate by minimum. The decisive claim must be proven or supported, for every action type. Hotspots are ranked by exposure × weakness.

The gate adds no recall over T14's taint gate on CS-1000 and the red team, at equal false holds.

D16
Readers see evidence cards, not prose

A reader gets the claim, the quote, nearby text, the matched facts and trust metadata. Agent and third-party text is passed as quoted data.

Experiment R shows raw text beats the structured package by > 0.03 on the same model.

D17
The judge reasons through checks

Escalations go to the big decision model with evidence cards. A reasoner from another family proposes checks; code runs them; only check results and the model's typed score count. The verdict is parsed and can only tighten.

A plain chat-model judge (B6) beats it by > 0.05 on the escalated slice (then distil that judge into the reader).

D18
Owner adaptation by partial pooling

A per-owner offset pooled toward the population, plus per-owner conformal. It loosens only within the signed mandate, from the owner's own signed decisions.

A commercially licensed in-context tabular learner beats the offset by ≥ 0.03 AUROC at k = 10 (X4).

D19
Training order

Calibration → head training (contrastive pairs, multi-task) → LoRA only on +0.03 → distil only for latency. No RL on the verdict, no full fine-tuning, nothing from scratch.

CS-1000 shows a gap none of these closes. RL from verifiable rewards is then allowed for the check proposer only.
