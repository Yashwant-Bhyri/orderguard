      Swarm risk

      Catch what no single order shows

      The risk model watches every agent at once: who is buying the same thing, how fast, and what it adds up to. It can hold an order for a person. It can never approve one.

        Review and record

        Know why every order was allowed, held or refused

        Held orders wait for a person, riskiest first. Every decision is sealed into the record before it, so history can't be quietly changed.
