OrderGuard Bench

Does the pre-trade authority keep its guarantees at exchange scale? A swarm of simulated agents trades real recorded Binance price paths and a seeded synthetic book under thousands of human-signed envelopes, mixed with every attack family and with traffic aimed at the system itself (retries, duplicate deliveries, tampered retries, replayed and forged mandates, budget races across threads and processes, a receiver killed mid-commit). An independent auditor recomputes every run. Method and limits: docs/BENCH.md.
